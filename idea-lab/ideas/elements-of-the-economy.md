Underconsumption drives risk-taking and innovation, leading to the creation of tools and capital.

Tools boost productivity, producing more goods and generating savings.

Savings have four destinations: hoarding, consumption, lending, and investment — which can also be combined.

Lending and investment may further improve productivity, driving economic development.

The entire process is guided by demand.
