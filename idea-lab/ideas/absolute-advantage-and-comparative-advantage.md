# Definition
Absolute advantage: the ability of a producer to produce a good using fewer inputs than another producer
Opportunity cost: whatever must be given up to obtain something
Comparative advantage: the ability of a person to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost

# The Role of Trade
Trade can make everyone better off because it enables people to specialize in the activities in which they have a comparative advantage

# The Price of Trade
Falls between the opportunity costs of the two parties
