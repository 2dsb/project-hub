# Vocabulary Priming Before Topic Conversation

## The Tactic

Before a conversation or writing session in a specific topic domain, pre-load ~30/50/100 common vocabulary words for that domain. Then use them in the session.

## Hypothesis

Priming the vocabulary reduces the cognitive load during the actual conversation — you're not searching for words mid-sentence; they're already warmed up in working memory. The fluency gain might be disproportionate to the prep effort.

## Uncertainty

- Does this actually work, or is it just a comforting ritual?
- Is 30 words enough, or does it need to be closer to 100+ to feel the effect?
- Does the priming decay within minutes, or does it last through a full session?
- Would it be better to prime **before** the session (pre-load) or **after** (review what you struggled with)?

## Connection to Topic Space

Each topic space (near/far) could have an associated vocabulary bank. Over time, repeated sessions in the same domain naturally build the vocabulary — but deliberate priming might accelerate the early sessions when the domain is new.
