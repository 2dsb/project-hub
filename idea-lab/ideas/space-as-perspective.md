# Space as a Perspective

## Core Insight

Sometimes, shifting from a temporal/sequential frame to a **spatial frame** changes everything. Thinking in terms of "spaces" — bounded regions with positions, distances, neighborhoods, boundaries — provides a perspective that temporal thinking alone cannot.

## Why Space Works as a Lens

- **Simultaneity**: Time forces sequence (one thing after another). Space allows everything to coexist at once — you see the whole layout.
- **Distance**: In a space, you can ask "how far apart are these two things?" — a question that doesn't make sense in temporal terms.
- **Position**: You can locate where something is within a space, independent of when it arrived.
- **Boundaries**: Spaces have edges. You can ask "is this inside or outside the space?" — a clean binary that time rarely gives you.
- **Neighborhoods**: Things close together in a space share properties. This enables clustering and zoning without requiring causal relationships.

## Known Instances

| Space | Domain | What It Reveals |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **Topic space** | English Learning system | A bounded conversational domain — near (daily life) vs. far (abstract). Deliberately chosen, not drifted into. |
| **(M, A) state space** | Project structure model | Every project state is a point. Completion is a region. Distance from completion is measurable. |
| **Possibility space** | Knowledge-possibility idea | What you can conceive of doing is bounded by what you know exists. Expanding knowledge = expanding the space. |
| **Review space** | Four-layer quality model | 6 cross-layer comparisons + 4 internal checks = 10-dimensional review space. Complete coverage, no 11th dimension. |
| **Conversation space** | conversation-topic-flow | Topics move within a space defined by participants, context, and history. |

## Relationship to Flow-Based Thinking

Flow-based thinking (Source→Router→Sink) is fundamentally **temporal** — it's about movement over time. Space-based thinking is its complement: **spatial** — it's about positions, distances, and regions at a given moment. Both are needed.
