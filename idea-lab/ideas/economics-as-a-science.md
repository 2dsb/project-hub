As a science, economics employs the scientific method:
- The general pattern: propose a theory, then collect data, analyze the data, and test the theory
- The relationship between theory and observation: theory explains observations, observations test theory
- The data collected (which can be understood as the object of observation): natural data provided by history

# The Language of Economics: Assumptions and Models
Assumptions simplify complex realities, making the world easier to explain.
Models, built upon assumptions, are the means of describing theories.
