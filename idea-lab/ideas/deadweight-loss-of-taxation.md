# Deadweight Loss of Taxation

![[IMG_0157.jpeg]]Deadweight loss: the reduction in total surplus caused by market distortions.

Leads to inefficient resource allocation.

Cause of deadweight loss: taxes prevent buyers and sellers from realizing the benefits of certain trades.

Influencing factor: price elasticity of supply and demand (the greater the elasticity, the greater the deadweight loss).

The higher the tax, the greater the deadweight loss.

As taxes increase, tax revenue first rises then falls.

Taxes distort incentives, causing greater potential losses.
