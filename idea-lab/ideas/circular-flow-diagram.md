This model illustrates **how money circulates between households and firms through markets**.

# Definition

Factors of production: Firms use inputs such as labor, land, and capital (buildings, machinery) to produce goods and services. These inputs are called factors of production.

# Assumptions

Simplified to consist of only two types of decision-makers — firms and households.

# The Circular Flow Diagram Explained

Two markets:

- **Market for goods and services**: Firms sell, households buy.
- **Factor market (market for factors of production)**: Households sell, firms buy.

Firms use factors of production to produce goods and services.
Households own the factors of production and consume the goods and services that firms produce.

Flow of money: One dollar — a household purchases a good or service in the market for goods and services, the money flows to the firm. The firm then spends it in the factor market, paying wages (labor) or rent (land), and the money flows back to the household.
