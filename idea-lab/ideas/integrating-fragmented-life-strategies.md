# Integrating Fragmented Life Strategies

Many of my current habits (recording ideas, daily reviews, etc.) operate in isolation — they're "fragmented" life strategies. I want to integrate them into a unified system rather than letting each one fight its own battle. The core questions: What is the shared underlying logic across these strategies? How can they reinforce each other instead of competing for attention?
