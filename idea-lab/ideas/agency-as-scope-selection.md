# Agency as Scope Selection

**One sentence**: The contradiction between subjective agency (主观能动性) and determinism dissolves once agency is given a **scope**: agency is not a claim about the world's causation but a *stipulated boundary* — you declare a chosen subsystem "subjectively controllable," everything outside it determined. Determinism and agency then hold together because they answer different questions.

## The Contradiction

Determinism, from physics:

- Without quantum mechanics, everything is determined: `S(t) → S(t+1)`.
- With quantum mechanics, random variables enter that are *subjectively uncontrollable* — quantum noise supplies no freedom.

Under this picture, 主观能动性 appears impossible: if every event is determined (or random), there is nothing for an agent to steer — "决定论下，我什么都改变不了". The two seem mutually exclusive: either the world is determined and agency is an illusion, or agency is real and determinism must be false.

## The Solution: Agency Needs a Scope

主观能动性 does not need to be a claim about the whole world — it needs a **范围** (scope). The move has three parts:

1. **Freedom is not discovered; it is stipulated.** We do not find a gap in causation; we *assume* a subsystem is subjectively controllable:
   - **Scope = thinking**: "thoughts are controllable" — agency holds across all of 思维.
   - **Scope = decisions within thinking**: "only the decision-part of thoughts is controllable" — the rest of thinking is determined (or plus subjectively-uncontrollable random variables).
2. **Everything outside the scope is determined** (+ subjectively-uncontrollable random variables). The scope boundary is the only place control lives.
3. **The scope can be drawn arbitrarily, and can move over time.** A natural partition: during the deliberation interval — from starting to deliberate to reaching a decision — thoughts are agentic; when not deliberating, thoughts are deterministic. This matches felt experience: steering thoughts while deciding, thoughts "just happening" otherwise (habit, autopilot — outside the scope, which is why they feel "not really me").

So 主观能动性 is a 范围选择 — a modeling commitment, not a property of reality. (It is stipulation, not the compatibilist *analysis* of freedom.)

## Why the Contradiction Dissolves

Determinism and agency answer different questions:

| Claim | Question it answers | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Determinism | How does the world run? | `S(t) → S(t+1)`, plus uncontrollable random |
| Agency (the scope) | Whose hands is the steering in? | "This step is mine to steer" |

Different kinds of claims cannot conflict. The agent's step need not be *undetermined* to be *owned* — even a fully determined deliberation can be "mine." The model is not "deterministic OR free"; it is deterministic, with a stipulated region the agent claims as its own.
