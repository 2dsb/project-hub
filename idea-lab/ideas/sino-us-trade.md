Contract: China exchanges gold for US dollars
```
China: gives gold to the US
US: gives dollars to China
```

Contract: China exchanges goods for US dollars
```
China: gives goods to the US
US: gives dollars to China
```

Effects:
- China's manufacturing booms, US services boom
- Americans can consume more than they produce; Chinese must consume less than they produce
- US inflation falls, US interest rates decline
