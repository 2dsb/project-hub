The government can enact policies to change/control certain factors:

Action: announce the benchmark interest rate.

```
政府：公布基准利率
```

The Federal Reserve is an extension of the U.S. Treasury. Its policy:

```
政策：公布基准利率

原理：经济学家运用集体智慧，推算出特定时段最理想的利率水平，使经济正常运行
比如，美联储可以降低利率，让企业或个人容易借贷，以刺激不景气的经济；
经济繁荣时，应该提高利率，让人们在借贷时三思而后行

影响：
低利率刺激借贷，抑制储蓄；并向借款人传达错误的信号，即经济状况良好，投资可行

缺陷：
1. 美联储的决定基于政治考量而非经济因素
2. 前提：美联储的人要比市场更清楚什么是恰当的利率水平
```

**Policy:** Announce the benchmark interest rate.

**Principle:** Economists use collective wisdom to calculate the optimal interest rate for a given period, allowing the economy to function normally. For example, the Fed can lower interest rates to make it easier for businesses and individuals to borrow, thereby stimulating a sluggish economy. When the economy is booming, rates should be raised to make people think twice before borrowing.

**Impact:** Low interest rates stimulate borrowing and suppress saving; they also send a false signal to borrowers that the economy is in good shape and investments are viable.

**Flaws:**
1. The Fed's decisions are based on political considerations rather than economic factors.
2. The premise: the Fed's people know better than the market what the appropriate interest rate level is.
