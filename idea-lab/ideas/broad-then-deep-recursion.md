**Information and connection advance together.** They are coupled, not sequential. To gain a connection you need pre-research — the information that lets you hold a real conversation with the target — and that same information is what you wanted for yourself anyway. So information feeds connection (pre-research opens the door), and connection feeds information (a person's first-hand view is exactly the information web-search can't reach). Each compounds through the other.

**Broad-then-deep, applied recursively.** Field selection follows a breadth-first-then-depth shape that recurses:

- **Broad** — spread the knowledge surface (AI, CS, embodied intelligence, math; possibly business too) while simultaneously spreading connections (talented people across different fields).
- **Commit** — choose one field.
- **Recurse** — inside that field, repeat the move: gather information and connections across its sub-directions, commit to one, and descend again.

So selection is a recursive descent down a tree: at each level you hold a broad map plus a broad set of connections, then narrow to one branch and repeat one level down. This is the "draw the map, then dive deep" shape — but it operates on information and connection together, and it descends into ever-narrower sub-fields rather than cycling over a single fixed map.

**Scope.** This sits at the information level (information + connection). Endorsement, platform, and channel are a different part — not less important, just outside this pattern.
