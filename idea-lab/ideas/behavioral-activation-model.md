# Behavioral Activation Model: Low Scores Are Starting Conditions, Not Static Labels

## Core Insight

Morning energy scores (mental/physical) are a **starting condition**, not a fixed label. However low they are in the morning, they can increase through behavioral activation — socializing, executing tasks, etc. The score is mutable, not static.

The previous low-energy day design treated low scores as static → adapted the plan downward immediately. This was wrong. The correct design treats low scores as a **transient state** and offers a behavioral activation path: push through for ~3h, then reassess. The score may have already recovered.

## Trigger: Social Accountability

Experience: Allergic rhinitis day, ~40/40 (below threshold). Friend invited me out, expected me at my best — had to pretend full energy, study together all day without him noticing.

- First ~3h: pure torture
- After ~3h: energy gradually improved
- End of day: 70/70 (above baseline)
- Friend never noticed anything was wrong

**Key finding**: Social accountability (a friend expecting you at your best) is the highest-leverage recovery trigger. Way more effective than solo endurance.

## Distinction

Genuine sickness (fever, injury) ≠ transient low energy that feels permanent but isn't. The skill needs a way to tell them apart — or at least an escape hatch from the downward-only path.

## Open Questions

- What specific behavioral activations work beyond social accountability?
- Would solo task initiation have the same effect? (Hypothesis: slower, rougher, but still works)
- What's the mechanism? Distraction? Momentum? Social pressure overriding internal signals?
