# Anki Cards from English Conversations

## The Intention

Turn English conversation output (phrases, patterns, corrections, vocabulary) into Anki cards. Maintain constant contact with the material through spaced repetition.

## Deeper Significance

This is the moment where "memory" stops being something that happens to you and becomes something you **design for**.

In the (M, A) model: Anki cards are an **artifact (A) whose sole function is to maintain M_explicit against decay**. Unlike reconnection docs (which enable rapid *recovery* of M after a gap), Anki cards *prevent* the decay from happening in the first place — they keep M warm through scheduled micro-exposures.

| Artifact Type | Strategy | Time Pattern |
|---------------|----------|--------------|
| Reconnection doc | Compress + fast decode | Long gaps → rapid reload |
| Anki cards | Spaced micro-exposure | Continuous low-dose maintenance |

Two complementary strategies for the same problem: M decays when you're not looking.
