Compare two types of people: one is swayed by common sense, the other recognizes, questions, and thinks about common sense from new angles.
What does the former become in real life? And the latter?

The former, swayed by common sense, essentially has no thoughts of their own — their ideas come from social indoctrination. In companies or organizations, because their ideas are shaped by the organization, they can never feel dissatisfied or offer constructive suggestions. If they possess some "social skills," they often become guardians of the status quo.

The latter questions common sense — they doubt what society has instilled in them. When you recognize the errors in common sense, you naturally develop critical views about the status quo.
