For Sinopia:

- Many businesses went bankrupt, and the economy fell into chaos.
- Soon, other businesses quickly emerged, producing things the Sinopians truly needed.
- The living standards of Sinopians began to gradually improve — production rose, consumption increased, and prices fell.

In Usonia, inflation intensified and productivity declined.

Later, Sinopians used "real fish" to purchase goods. Their consumer power surpassed that of native Usonians, causing goods to flow into Sinopia.
