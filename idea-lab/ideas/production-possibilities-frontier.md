# Definition

A graph showing the various combinations of output that an economy can produce given the available factors of production and production technology.

If an economy is getting all it can from its scarce resources (points on the production possibilities frontier), the outcome is called efficient.

# Shifts of the Production Possibilities Frontier

Suppose an economy produces two goods, A and B. If a technological advance improves the production efficiency of A, the production possibilities frontier shifts outward.

# Relationship to the Ten Principles

- Principle 1: Choosing a point on the production possibilities frontier involves a trade-off.
- Principle 2: Moving along the production possibilities frontier — producing more of A — incurs an opportunity cost of forgoing some amount of B, which is tied to the slope at that point.
