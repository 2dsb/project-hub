# The Process of Knowledge Production

The Zettelkasten method involves continuing to explore based on existing notes, following your interests. Through extensive reading, new understandings and reflections emerge, which in turn produce new notes.

Liberal education investigates "the process of knowledge production," and the Zettelkasten approach described above aligns precisely with this actual process.

# The Role of Connection

The humanities differ from media like advertising in that they demand reflection. Reflection means building on deep feeling and thinking about art, then considering its connection to life. This shares a striking similarity with how permanent notes function in the Zettelkasten system.

# Shared Goals

The goal of liberal education is to cultivate students' ability to "think beyond time and space." More concretely, when facing a specialized problem, they learn to approach it from multiple domains and perspectives. Sometimes, fleeting notes arise precisely from explaining everyday phenomena through different theoretical lenses — this is how multi-perspective thinking is developed. Both the Zettelkasten method and liberal education cut across all disciplines.
