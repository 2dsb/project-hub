# Clarity as a Universal Principle

Treat "describe it clearly" as a universal principle: whether crafting natural-language narration of text structure, recording ideas, or summarizing retrospectives — always pursue clearer, more precise description. This is not a local methodology confined to one domain, but a cross-domain meta-principle.
