# The Three Definitions of Technology
1. Technology is a means to fulfill human purposes (e.g., a speech recognition algorithm).
2. Technology is the integration of practices and components.
      For example, electronics and biotechnology are the integration or toolbox of many technologies or practices (better called "bodies of technology").
3. Technology is the collection of devices and engineering practices deployed in a given culture ("technology" accelerates our lives).
## Different Angles
\#1 Technology in the singular sense
\#2 Technology in the plural sense
\#3 Technology in the general sense

# The Value of Distinguishing Definitions
These three definitions approach "technology" from different angles, and each forms and develops differently.
- Technology in the singular sense (e.g., the steam engine) emerges as a new concept and develops by modifying its internal components.
- Technology in the plural sense (e.g., electricity) is built around certain phenomena and devices, developing through the modification of its constructs and practices.
- Technology in the general sense arises from the application of natural phenomena and grows organically as new elements, formed from combinations of old elements, emerge.
