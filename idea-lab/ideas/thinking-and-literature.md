Full title: **From the Nature of Thinking as Cleansing Common Sense, to How Literature Can Improve Thinking Ability — Using *A Single Sentence Is Worth Ten Thousand* as an Example**

# 1. Thinking is the process of cleansing common sense

Common sense consists of ideas instilled in us by society — beliefs we unconsciously hold without ever having examined them, ranging from worldview and social outlook to self-perception.

The purpose of thinking is to identify these beliefs and re-examine them.

# 2. How to think? (How to discover socially instilled beliefs and re-examine them?)

Relying solely on yourself is impossible, because you always believe your own views are correct (that is what common sense is — beliefs you hold unconsciously). When confronted with an opinion, if it is just a dead proposition, it is easy to refute and dismiss. But when that opinion becomes another person's position, refuting it requires refuting the person — the best outcome is that one side gets persuaded.

So we need the help of others:
- Discuss topics with teachers to learn new perspectives and ways of thinking
- Build consensus with classmates and then overturn it
- Read literary works to see how predecessors understood the world and the self
- ...

# 3. Key insights on the nature of common sense and implications for cleansing it

We should not treat the beliefs instilled by common sense as a vague, undifferentiated whole.

On the contrary, what common sense instills encompasses different dimensions — worldview, self-perception, and more.

This suggests that thinking — i.e., cleansing common sense — must also proceed from these different angles.

# 4. Taking *A Single Sentence Is Worth Ten Thousand* as an example

First, it should be noted that, as discussed in [Liberal Arts Education and Art](../permanent-notes/9-博雅教育与艺术.md), literature carefully observes human psychology and social phenomena.

At what levels can we use this to cleanse common sense?

- **At the human nature level**: we understand how humans think and behave (because these belong to human nature, they do not change over time, and they hold relevance for the present)
- **At the social level**: we understand rural social structures, etc.
- **At the personal level**: we… [Excellent Sheep C8.1](../literature-notes/优秀的绵羊c8.1.md)
    - Discover oneself through others
    - Understand condensed versions of lives never lived, providing material for deep reflection and imagining alternative ways of living (imagination for life)
