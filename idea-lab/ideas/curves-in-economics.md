# Slope
Represents the degree to which one quantity responds to another.

# Cause and Effect
Distinguish between correlation and causality.

If two variables are correlated, one may have influenced the other, or the relationship could involve **reverse causation** (cigarette lighters and cancer), or both may be driven by a third, **omitted variable** (police and crime).
