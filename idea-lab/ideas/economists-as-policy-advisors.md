# The Difference Between Policy Advisors and Scientists

Scientists describe society; policy advisors attempt to improve it.

## Positive and Normative Statements

There are two ways to make claims about the world: positive statements and normative statements.

- Positive statements are descriptive — they describe the world as it is. We can confirm or refute positive statements by examining evidence.
- Normative statements are prescriptive claims about how the world should be. Evaluating them involves not only facts but also values.

Examples:

- Positive statement: Minimum wage laws cause unemployment.
- Normative statement: The government should raise the minimum wage.

# Consensus and Disagreement Among Economists as Policy Advisors

Disagreement: Stems from differences in values, such as the appropriate level of taxation.

Economists agree on many things.

Take rent control as an example: economists believe that rent control has adverse effects on housing availability and quality.
