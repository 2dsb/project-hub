Information exchange during meals splits into two modes: broad-but-shallow vs. narrow-but-deep. Broad-but-shallow: wide scope, but the initiator often lacks a clear grasp of the big picture — unprepared narration with no focus or logical thread, leaving the listener confused. Narrow-but-deep: tight scope, but the initiator understands the topic from macro structure down to details — information transfers more effectively and precisely.
