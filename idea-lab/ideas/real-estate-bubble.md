1. Banks discover that "providing mortgages" is a great business.
2. Banks lower mortgage interest rates; investor capital keeps pouring in.
3. Demand for housing increases.
4. Housing prices rise, supply increases; this in turn creates housing "speculators."
5. Suddenly, one house fails to sell, causing the borrower to default on the loan.
6. As a result, everyone suddenly wants to sell, supply exceeds demand, and housing prices crash.
7. Defaults rise, bad debt piles up; investor confidence drops; other industries are impacted, and unemployment rises.
