1. Providing aid and stimulus policies:
   - Offering assistance to homebuyers
   - Building infrastructure to boost employment
   - Developing alternative energy (the donkey-powered device), which in turn boosted manufacturing in the Middle Island Empire (Sinopia)
2. The problem: this requires printing more money, but Usonia no longer has any fish reserves, which could trigger inflation.
   - They have only 3 choices:
     1. Reduce consumption
     2. Increase production
     3. Exchange dollars with foreign countries for goods
3. The US chose the third option, which led to two outcomes:
   - On one hand, a stronger dollar made foreign goods cheaper, causing domestic manufacturers to lose competitiveness
   - On the other hand, Americans could sustain a consume-more-than-produce lifestyle; bank savings grew, interest rates fell, fueling more borrowing and consumption
4. Further potential consequences:
   - America's creditor nations, such as China and India, may stop purchasing US Treasury bonds, leading to:
     - Creditor nations redirecting the fruits of their labor to their own citizens
     - Very low domestic savings in the US, causing the dollar's purchasing power to decline and inflation to take hold
