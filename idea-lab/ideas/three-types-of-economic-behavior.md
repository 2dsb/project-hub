Whether it's an individual, a company, a bank, or a venture capital firm, there seem to be only two types of contracts:

1. Loan
2. Investment

Let X and Y be economic agents.

```
Loan:
At time A, X gives Y capital of 10.
At time B, Y gives X capital of 10 + t.
```

```
Investment:
At time A, X gives Y capital of 10.
If condition B is met in the future, Y gives X capital of 20.
If condition B is not met in the future, Y gives X nothing.
```
