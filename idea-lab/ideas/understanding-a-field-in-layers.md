To understand a domain's information, you don't need to know everything — you need to know its layers. For graphics and vision, that's exactly three:

- **Math layer (bottom)** — the underlying mathematical stack. Rendering = Monte Carlo / probability, geometry processing = differential geometry / topology, vision = statistics / optimization / signal processing, 3DGS = optimization / numerical methods.
- **Tech layer (middle)** — tools, papers, and implementations: Mitsuba, gsplat, nerfstudio, NeRF / 3DGS, UE5 Nanite / Lumen.
- **Business layer (top)** — companies, funding, and commercialization: VAST/Tripo, Meshy, HeyGen, and the current shift from asset generation toward world models.

The method generalizes. To understand any sub-field, the best approach is to layer it. The specific layers may not be these exact three, but a field can always be decomposed into layers — and that decomposition is the fastest route to an overview.
