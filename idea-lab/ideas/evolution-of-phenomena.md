# How Are Phenomena Captured (Discovered)?
## At the Logical Level
Phenomena cluster together and can be categorized by function into phenomenon families (e.g., electrical phenomena).
Once a phenomenon is discovered, other phenomena in the same family become easier to discover.
## At the Physical Level
The process by which scientists **discover anomalies** and define them is "phenomenon capture."

Common scenarios for discovering anomalies:
1. Revisiting an experiment and noticing overlooked details
2. Finding clues during theoretical derivation
3. Byproducts of trial and error
## The Role of Technology in Phenomenon Capture
Discovered phenomena are domesticated into technology, technology produces equipment, and equipment is used to discover new phenomena.
