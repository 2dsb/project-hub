# life-console — Daily Operations

Three files:

- **profile.md**: Personal info, health conditions, medications, preferences. **Written in Chinese and keep it Chinese** — it's meant to be shown to others (e.g., doctors, contacts), so do not translate it to English.
- **reminders.md**: Time-based reminders. Add, check on session start, soft-delete (set `active: false`).
- **schedule.md**: Fixed recurring schedule. Match today's day_of_week or date=today's block. Alert on time-range overlaps.
