# Outreach Message Templates

Templates for the first WeChat message after meeting someone at an event. All assume a seniority gap (age, career stage, status) and that you exchanged contacts in person.

---

## Scenario A: They Messaged First (Reply Mode)

**When**: The other person sent an intro or greeting first. You're replying.

```
很荣幸认识您。今天和您交流真的受益良多。
[Identity anchor — one sentence, woven naturally]
以后希望能有机会多向您请教。
```

**Phone number — only if they gave theirs first.** If they included their number in their intro, reciprocate with yours. If they didn't, skip it. WeChat is already a complete contact channel; an extra phone number adds nothing unless it's a reply to theirs.

### Why This Works

- You have the "reply" context — they already acknowledged you, so warmth is natural
- `受益良多` ties back to the real conversation you had

---

## Scenario B: You're the First Mover (They Stayed Silent)

**When**: You added each other at the event, but they haven't sent anything. You need to break the ice.

```
很高兴今天在长江认识您。我是北大数院准大一新生，今天跟我父亲一起过来学习。
以后希望有机会多向您请教。
```

### Why This Works

- `长江` instantly anchors the context — he knows where he knows you from
- Identity anchor is the same sentence as the greeting — no wasted space. Critical: he may have added 30 people that day
- Two lines only. Shorter than his silence is
- No false intimacy — you're not pretending the conversation was deeper than it was

### What NOT to Do (First Mover)

- **Don't ask "还记得我吗"** — awkward if he doesn't. The identity anchor handles it naturally
- **Don't apologize** — "不好意思打扰了" signals low confidence. You met in person, you were invited to add him, you have every right to message
- **Don't send a wall of text** — two lines, clean exit. Let him reply before you add more

## Scenario C: Brief Encounter (Can't Say "受益良多")

**When**: You met briefly, didn't have a real conversation, so "受益良多" would be dishonest. Keep it clean.

```
很高兴今天在长江认识您。我是北大数院准大一新生，今天跟我父亲一起过来学习。
以后希望有机会多向您请教。
```

Same as Scenario B — with an optional variant if there was at least a shared moment:

```
今天在长江时间仓促，没能多聊，但很高兴认识您。我是北大数院准大一新生。
以后希望有机会多向您请教。
```

`时间仓促，没能多聊` — acknowledges the briefness honestly. Then identity anchor, then door open.

### If They Spoke On Stage (But You Didn't Talk 1-on-1)

```
今天在长江听了您的分享，很受启发。我是北大数院准大一新生。
以后希望有机会多向您请教。
```

`听了您的分享，很受启发` is honest — you *did* listen, you *were* inspired. Doesn't require a personal conversation.

## Core Principles (All Scenarios)

### Identity Anchor

The line most people skip. Formula: **Who + Why you were there + Context**

> 我是北大数院准大一新生，今天跟我父亲过来学习。

- PKU math = high-status, memorable
- "准大一" = honest about age, frames it as hunger
- "跟我父亲过来" = explains presence, signals family values

**Shelf life**: at 18, "my father brought me" works. By 20, evolve to your own agency: "I'm researching X and wanted to learn from people in the field."

### Why 请教, Not 指导

| Word | Signal | Pressure on them |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| `请教` | I want to learn from you casually | Low — occasional questions are fine |
| `指导` | I want you to mentor me formally | Higher — implies ongoing responsibility |

At 18, `请教` reads as mature and self-aware. `指导` feels like asking them to officially take you on. Same intent, lighter touch.

### Universal Don'ts

- **Don't skip the identity anchor** — no matter how good the conversation was, they met many people
- **Don't use `有幸可以认识您`** — the `可以` softens it unnecessarily. You *did* meet them. `有幸认识您` or `很荣幸认识您` or `很高兴认识您`
- **Don't overclaim the interaction** — if you barely talked, don't say "受益良多." `很高兴认识您` is honest and sufficient
- **Don't ask for anything** — no favors, introductions, or advice. First message is about opening the door, not walking through it
- **Don't send a wall of text** — 2-4 lines max

### Follow-Up (24-48 Hours Later)

If they reply warmly, the follow-up window opens. Reference something specific from your original conversation — not generic "nice to meet you" again. Prove you were listening and are still thinking about it.
